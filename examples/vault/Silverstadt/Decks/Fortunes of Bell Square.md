# Fortunes of Bell Square

The fortune teller under the bell tower lays one card for every copper. Cards go back into her deck, except the Cracked Bell.

- [ ] The Lantern — Someone you trust keeps a light burning for you.
- [ ] The Drowned Coin — Money comes, and leaves just as fast.
- [ ] The Heron — Patience pays: the next long rest restores an extra Hit Die.
- [ ] The Cracked Bell — An old promise breaks.
  The card crumbles in her hand and leaves the deck.
- [ ] Midnight — She sweeps the cards together. No more fortunes tonight.
