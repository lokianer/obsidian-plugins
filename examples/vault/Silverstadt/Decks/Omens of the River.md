# Omens of the River

Draw one when the party crosses the river at night. The first task list is the deck: an open item is still in it, a ticked one has been drawn.

- [ ] A heron stands on the third lantern and does not fly off
- [ ] The water below the second arch is warm
- [x] Bells under the water, faint, out of rhythm
- [ ] A drowned ledger floats up, open at a page with the party's names
- [ ] [[Elder Wren]] on the bank, waving them back
- [x] Every lantern gutters at once, then steadies
- [ ] Ink in the river, a long dark ribbon heading upstream
- [ ] Nothing. The river is too quiet.
