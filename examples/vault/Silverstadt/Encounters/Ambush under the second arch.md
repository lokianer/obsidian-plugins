# Ambush under the second arch

If the party tries to sell the charts behind Mira's back, the Court answers here: the second arch of the [[Lantern Bridge]], low tide, mud to the ankles.

## Terrain

- Mud: difficult terrain everywhere below the arch
- The tide comes in after round 5: each round after, the water rises a foot
- The bridge bell: anyone who rings it (action) brings the night watch in 1d4 rounds

## Tactics

The thugs pin, the sharpshooters stay on the ledge (half cover). [[Sable]] wants the charts, not a fight; she runs at half hit points.

> [!gm]
> If [[Sergeant Pell]] is on watch, the watch arrives in 1 round instead.
