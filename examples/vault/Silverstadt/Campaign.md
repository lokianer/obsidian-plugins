# Silverstadt

A city campaign. Silverstadt sits where the river Selle meets a tidal estuary, and everything in it is about tides, bells and records: who owns what, who owes what, who wrote it down and where the writing went. The party arrived in [[Silverstadt]] looking for work on the docks and has, over thirteen sessions, become mixed up with all three of the city's factions without joining any of them.

This note has `type: campaign`, so the plugin is active for this folder and everything below it.

## Structure of this folder

| Folder | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| `Factions/` | One folder per faction, holding its `factions.md` and its members. A faction folder inside another is a sub-faction |
| `NPCs/` | Characters who belong to no faction |
| `Party/` | The player characters (`type: character`) |
| `Places/` | Districts, buildings and landmarks |
| `Quests/` | Open and closed threads |
| `Sessions/` | One log per session |
| `Encounters/` | Prepared fights for the battle tracker |
| `Tables/`, `Decks/` | Things to roll on and draw from at the table |

## The factions

- [[Order of the Lantern/factions|Order of the Lantern]] — keeps the bridge and the river
- [[The Archivists/factions|The Archivists]] — keep the city's records
- [[The Gutter Court/factions|The Gutter Court]] — the thieves, with [[The Bench/factions|the Bench]] inside it
- [[The Drowned Choir/factions|The Drowned Choir]] — a cult; its note is full of deliberately broken values for testing

## The party

- [[Tessaly Vane]], human fighter (battle master), carries the map.
- [[Orrin Quill]], halfling bard (lore), the party's face and the reason they know [[Mira Voss]].
- [[Dagny]], goliath barbarian, hates boats, currently owns one.
- [[Ilex]], wood elf ranger (gloom stalker), keeps the ledger of who owes the party favours.

## Current state (after session 14)

The party holds the tidewater charts recovered in [[Session 12 – The Sunken Archive]]. [[Aldric Thane]] suspects them of the fire at the [[Archive]]. In [[Session 14 – Bell Square]] Mira finally named her price.

> [!gm] The shape of the campaign
> Under the Sunken Archive sleeps whatever the bridge lanterns keep asleep. The Order lights them without knowing why, the Archivists want to reach it, Mira wants to own the knowledge. The party will decide who gets there first.
