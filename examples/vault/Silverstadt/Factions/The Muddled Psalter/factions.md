# The Muddled Psalter

A hymnal society that copies the [[The Drowned Choir/factions|Drowned Choir]]'s songs and gets every verse wrong. They admire the Choir from a distance, buy old hymn sheets from the [[The Archivists/factions|Archivists]], and have never forgiven the [[Order of the Lantern/factions|Order of the Lantern]].

The Psalter has no members yet; opening it one level deeper shows an empty faction.

> [!gm]
> One of their wrong verses is the right one. Nobody has noticed.
