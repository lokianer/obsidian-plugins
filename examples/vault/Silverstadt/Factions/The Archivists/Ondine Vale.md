# Ondine Vale

First Keeper of [[The Archivists/factions|The Archivists]]. Has not met the party and intends to keep it that way. Three hundred years old, by her own account; the Archive's oldest catalogue is in her hand.

## What she wants

The Archive's debt to [[Mira Voss]] erased — and the Court with it.

> [!gm]
> She knows the debt is forged. She cannot prove it without admitting what the Archive deposited with Mira in the first place.
