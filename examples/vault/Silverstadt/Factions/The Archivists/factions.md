# The Archivists

Keepers of Silverstadt's records, above and below the water. Thirty members, most of them clerks; three Keepers. They own the [[Archive]] on Bell Square and the warehouse cellar that leads to the [[Sunken Archive]].

## Goals

To recover the lower stacks and the vault beneath them. [[Corvin Marsh]], the Second Keeper, runs that effort and hired the party for it. The First Keeper, [[Ondine Vale]], disapproves of adventurers on principle.

## Methods

Patience, pumps and payment. The Archivists do not fight; they wait, and they keep records of who wronged them. Their oath is "written, kept, found".

- [[Order of the Lantern/factions|Order of the Lantern]]: courtesy on top, suspicion underneath
- [[The Gutter Court/factions|The Gutter Court]]: they buy stolen records back from [[Sable]] without asking where she got them
- The Archive owes [[Mira Voss]] money, and no Keeper will explain why
