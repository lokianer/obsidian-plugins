# Isolde Ferrant

Commander of the [[Order of the Lantern/factions|Order of the Lantern]]. Met by the party once, briefly, and that was enough. Silver hair cropped short, a burn scar across one hand she never explains. She holds no opinion that is not also the Order's — this note has no `relations` of its own, so she inherits every value from her faction.

## What she wants

The charter. She is the only living member who knows it exists.

> [!gm] revealed: 15
> Ferrant has read a copy of the first page. It names the thing under the river.
