# The Gutter Court

The thieves of Lantern Quay and the Undercroft, organised loosely enough that they can deny being organised at all. "Court" because disputes are settled by a sitting of the senior members in a drained cistern.

## Structure

- [[The Bench/factions|The Bench]]: five seniors, a sub-faction with its own folder inside this one
- The Purse: [[Mira Voss]], though she would deny it in public
- Runners, fences, night porters: [[Sable]] is the one the party deals with

## Goals

Money, obviously, but also the tidewater charts. The Bench thinks the charts are a smuggling route. Mira knows better.

## Territory

Lantern Quay back streets, the Undercroft, the second arch of the [[Lantern Bridge]]. Not the Bellward; the Order sees to that.
