# Lantern Ward

The Order of the Lantern's own ward. A ring of pale light, 10 feet across, rises around a point you choose within range. Undead and fiends take 2d6 radiant damage when they enter it for the first time on a turn, and every creature inside has advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

## At Higher Levels

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the radiant damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 2nd.
