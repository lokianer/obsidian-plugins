# Street Rumours

Three rollable tables for the dice module. A heading naming a die makes the first list or table under it rollable.

## Rumours on Lantern Quay (d6)

- The bridge lanterns were lit at noon last Tuesday, and nobody will say why
- [[Mira Voss]] bought the burnt Archive's east wing for one silver
- A Keeper was seen rowing out to the reed beds at night
- The Gutter Court has a new chair, and she wears red
- Ink eels have been found in the harbour, miles from any archive
- [[Brannoc Ironweld]] has stopped talking to his machines
