# Tideborn

Silverstadt's river folk: born in the tidal quarter, said to have a drop of the drowned city in them. An own race for **New NPC** — pick it in the race field; its traits go into the stat block, its names into the dice.

## Traits

- **Amphibious.** Breathes air and water.
- **Tide Sense.** Always knows the hour of the tide and whether it is rising.
- **Bell-Deaf.** Advantage on saving throws against being deafened or charmed by sound.
