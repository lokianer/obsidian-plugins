# Dice R3 — Stat Blocks and Characters — Implementation Plan

**Status:** built 2026-10-26, accepted in Obsidian on 2026-10-26 ("es läuft alles") (the maintainer: "weiter" after R2).

**Goal:** Roll from every stat block view (attacks, damage, saves, skills) and roll the party's
saves and skills from the Dice tab and the Battle tab's character cards — on the dice stage, with a
log line that names who rolled what.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-dice-design.md` §1 (dice in stat blocks), §5 and §7
(phase R3).

## Review Focus

1. **Rollable parts of an entry** (`src/dice/statblock.ts`, pure): an attack bonus (`+7 to hit`,
   `Attack Roll: +7`, German `+7 auf Treffer`, `Angriffswurf: +7`) rolls `d20+7`; a damage
   expression (`10 (1d10 + 5)`, German `10 (1W10 + 5)`) rolls the dice in brackets. Saving throw
   DCs are the target's roll and stay text.
2. **Stat blocks:** in notes, the Monster Manual and the Battle tab the attack bonuses and damage
   expressions in entries and every save and skill are buttons. A click rolls; Shift-click with
   advantage, Alt-click with disadvantage (d20 rolls only); right-click or a long press offers
   Normal / Advantage / Disadvantage. The stage plays in a small window (by the Dice animation
   setting; off: a notice with the result). The label is "Owlbear: Beak" / "Owlbear: Beak ·
   damage".
3. **Characters** (`src/dice/checks.ts`, pure): `saves: { dex: 5 }` and `skills: { persuasion: 7 }`
   in a character note's frontmatter, else the saves and skills of the note's stat block. Names
   in the plugin's language on the buttons ("Überzeugen"), English in the log.
4. **The Dice tab:** a Party section — each character of the campaign's party with their saves and
   skills as buttons; they roll in the tab with its Normal / Advantage / Disadvantage and DC.
5. **The Battle tab:** a character's card shows the same buttons.
6. **Log lines**, for example:
   - `Orrin Quill rolls Persuasion: d20+7 → 12 (5, +7)`
   - `Owlbear rolls Beak: d20+7 → 15 (8, +7)`
   - `Owlbear rolls Beak damage: 1d10+5 → 9 (4, +5)`

   Rolls from stat blocks also join the Dice tab's history.

## Calls I made without the maintainer (check these)

- Saving throw DCs in entries are not buttons (the target rolls, often a player).
- Monster names and entry names go into the log as the block shows them (German with German
  Obsidian); the verb and the structure stay English.
- No critical-hit damage button: a crit is rolled as the damage twice (or with the dice doubled in
  the Dice tab's formula). Can come later.
- The monster builder's preview has no dice buttons (it is a preview).
- Ability checks are not buttons (the spec names attacks, damage, saves and skills).
