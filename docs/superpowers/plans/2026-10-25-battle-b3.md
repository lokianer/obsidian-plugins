# Battle Tracker B3 — Encounter Builder — Implementation Plan

**Status:** built 2026-10-25, accepted in Obsidian on 2026-10-26 ("es läuft alles") (the maintainer: "Weiter" after B2).

**Goal:** **New encounter** opens a builder in the New NPC dialog's style: the campaign's party
with its levels, a difficulty (Low, Moderate, High) and its XP budget, monsters of the Monster
Manual (SRD and own) as tiles with counts, the XP spent against the budget live beside it; it
writes a `type: encounter` note the Battle tab runs. From the Monster Manual, **Add to encounter**
puts a monster into an encounter note.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-battle-tracker-design.md` §4 and §6 (phase B3).

**Source checked:** the XP Budget per Character table of the SRD 5.2.1 (CC-BY-4.0, "Combat
Encounter Difficulty", Gameplay Toolbox), read from a Markdown conversion of the SRD 5.2.1 on
2026-10-25: levels 1–20, Low / Moderate / High, from 50 / 75 / 100 to 6,400 / 13,200 / 22,000. The
SRD 5.2.1 spends the budget as a plain sum of the creatures' XP — no group multipliers.

## Review Focus

1. **Budget** (`src/battle/budget.ts`, pure): the table; a party's budget is the sum of each
   character's entry (equal levels give the SRD's "entry × number of characters"); the rating of
   an encounter's XP: within the Low, Moderate or High budget, or beyond High.
2. **The note**: `type: encounter`, `difficulty`, optional `location`, `party: false` when the
   party is left out, `combatants` as `monster:` entries with `count` — the format B1 reads.
3. **Add to encounter**: the monster joins an encounter's `combatants` through
   `processFrontMatter`; the same monster again raises its `count`.
4. **The builder**: party chips (all on; without a party in the campaign, a number of characters
   and a level), three difficulty tiles with their budgets, monster tiles with a search, rows with
   counts, the preview with spent / budget / left and the rating; Create writes the note into the
   encounter folder (setting, `Encounters`) and shows it in the Battle tab.

## Calls I made without the maintainer (check these)

- Mixed party levels: each character's own budget, summed (the SRD only gives the equal case).
- Party levels come from the characters' `level` frontmatter; a character without one counts as
  level 1 and the builder says so.
- The builder writes monsters only; notes and written combatants are added in the note by hand.
- The rating names how hard the XP is (Low, Moderate, High, beyond High), whatever difficulty was
  picked; the picked difficulty sets the budget the bar fills.
