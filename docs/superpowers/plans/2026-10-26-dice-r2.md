# Dice R2 — Rollable Tables — Implementation Plan

**Status:** built 2026-10-26, awaiting the check in Obsidian (the maintainer: "weiter" after R1).

**Goal:** Roll the campaign's tables — a heading naming a die makes the first list or table under
it rollable — from the Dice tab and from `dice: [[Note#Heading]]` in notes, on the dice stage, with
the row that came up shown (links working) and offered for the session log.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-dice-design.md` §4 and §7 (phase R2).

## Review Focus

1. **Reading tables** (`src/dice/tables.ts`, pure): a heading of any level ending in a die in
   brackets (`(d6)`, `(1d8)`, `(2d6)`, `(W20)`) makes the first list or table under it, before the
   next heading, rollable. A list is numbered by its order; a table's first column holds numbers or
   ranges (`2–4`, `5-6`), the other cells are the row's text. A total that lands on no row (13–16
   in "Night on Lantern Quay") is a gap: recorded as it fell, never re-rolled.
2. **The Dice tab:** a Tables section with every `type: table` note of the campaign and its
   rollable headings as buttons; a click plays the stage with the heading as its label and the row
   below the total; the history keeps the row.
3. **`dice: [[Street Rumours#Night on Lantern Quay (d20)]]`** in notes (Reading view and Live
   Preview): a button with the heading; `[[Note]]` alone rolls the note's first table. A link to
   nothing rollable is still a button (the note may not be loaded yet) and shows a notice on click.
4. **Log line:** `Rolled Night on Lantern Quay (d20): 11 → [[Sable]] on a rooftop, pretending not
to see you`; a gap `… : 14 → no row`.

## Calls I made without the maintainer (check these)

- The die must end the heading, in brackets; a heading without one is no table.
- A table's text is all cells after the first, joined with " — " when there are several.
- Dice Roller's block references (`[[Note^id]]`) are not read; headings only.
