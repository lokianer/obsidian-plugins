# Battle Tracker B1 — The Tracker — Implementation Plan

**Status:** built 2026-10-25, accepted in Obsidian on 2026-10-26 ("es läuft alles").

**Goal:** A Battle tab that runs a fight from an encounter note: the party joins, initiative is
rolled and typed in, rounds and turns advance, hit points, temporary hit points, death saves,
conditions with durations and concentration are tracked, and the fight lives as a `## Combat` table
in the encounter note.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-battle-tracker-design.md` (reviewed 2026-10-25, every
call in its §8 accepted).

## Review Focus

1. **Encounter notes:** links, `monster:` entries, inline combatants with `count`, `party: false`;
   the example vault's provisional encounter reads unchanged — Task 1.
2. **Fight rules:** groups share a roll and are numbered; ties by Dexterity; turns skip the
   defeated; durations count down at the end of their holder's turn; temporary hit points absorb
   first; monsters at 0 are defeated, characters go down with death saves; healing a downed
   character brings them back; concentration reminds of DC max(10, half the damage) — Task 2.
3. **The `## Combat` table:** written from the fight and read back into the same fight; only that
   section changes — Task 3.
4. **The tab:** encounter picker, Start / End, initiative inputs and a roll button, Next / Previous
   turn, rows with HP bars and condition chips, the card with HP controls, conditions, death saves
   and the stat block — Task 4.
