# Deck of Many Things D1 — Standard deck — Implementation Plan

**Status:** built 2026-10-25; awaiting the maintainer's check in Obsidian.

**Goal:** A Deck tab that draws from the SRD's Deck of Many Things (22 cards, 13 as a setting) by
its rules, in English and German, with the deck's state and draw history in a note.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-deck-of-many-things-design.md` (reviewed 2026-10-25,
every call in its §8 accepted).

## Global Constraints

Root `CLAUDE.md`. SRD text only, attribution in README and NOTICE. Theme variables only, text at
least 16 px, works at phone width, no motion with reduced motion. Clean up before every commit,
then `pnpm check`.

## Review Focus

1. **Data:** 22 cards, 13 of them in the small deck (Sun, Moon, Star, Throne, Key, Knight, The Void,
   Flames, Skull, Ruin, Euryale, Rogue, Jester); every card has an English and German name and
   effect; the dice and numbers of the English survive in the German; no feet left — Task 1.
2. **Rules:** the Fool leaves and its redraw counts as the same declared draw; the Jester leaves and
   offers 10,000 XP or two more draws; the Idiot offers one more draw; The Void and Donjon end the
   drawing; every other card returns — Task 2.
3. **Note:** created on the first draw; a leaving card is ticked; one draw line per card appended
   under "Draws"; a card ticked by hand is out of the deck — Task 3.

**Source:** the 5e-bits SRD entry lacks six effects; the full SRD 5.1 text is taken from the Open5e
SRD data (`open5e-api`, `data/v1/wotc-srd/MagicItem.json`). Skull's avatar of death has no stat
block in either source; it is left out rather than typed from memory.
