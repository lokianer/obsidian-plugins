# Battle Tracker B2 — Log and Monster Manual — Implementation Plan

**Status:** built 2026-10-25, awaiting the check in Obsidian (the maintainer: "Mach weiter, ich teste später" after B1).

**Goal:** The fight reaches tonight's session note, and the Battle tab's line-up can change while
it runs: lines for the fight's start, a combatant defeated, down or dead, and its end with the XP
of the defeated enemies; **Add to combat** from the Monster Manual; adding a monster, a note or a
written combatant in the tab; removing one.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-battle-tracker-design.md` §3 and §6 (phase B2).

## Review Focus

1. **Log lines** (`src/battle/log.ts`, pure): what happened between two states of a fight —
   begins (round 0 → 1), defeated, down, dead, ends — as lines under `## Log` of tonight's session
   (`- 21:40 Combat begins: [[Ambush under the second arch]]`,
   `- 21:52 Round 3: Owlbear 1 defeated`, `- 21:58 Combat ends after 5 rounds — 1,400 XP`). The setting **Battle log**: ask
   (a notice with **Log → Session 14**, the default), automatic, or off. Lines are English, like
   the `## Combat` table.
2. **Mid-fight changes** (`fight.ts`): `addFighter` rolls the newcomer's initiative (a member of a
   group already fighting takes the group's number and the next member number) and puts it into
   the order without re-sorting what the GM nudged; `removeCombatant` passes the turn on when the
   removed one had it. Fighters gain `xp` (from the challenge rating) for the end line.
3. **Reading back:** a row in `## Combat` that the encounter note does not list (a combatant added
   mid-fight) is matched to a monster or a note by its name without the member number, so its
   stat block and XP come back after a restart.
4. **The tab and the Monster Manual:** an add row under the order (monster, note or name, with
   optional HP and AC), **Remove from fight** on the card, **Add to combat** on a Monster Manual
   card (into the open tab's running fight; a notice when none runs).

## Calls I made without the maintainer (check these)

- Log lines for begin, defeated, down, dead and end only — not for every round or every hit.
- The log lines are written in English in every language, like the `## Combat` table.
- The XP is the sum of the defeated enemies' XP by challenge rating (SRD table), unsplit.
- A newcomer from the Monster Manual joins the enemies; a member of a group already fighting
  shares its initiative.
