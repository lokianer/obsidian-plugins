# Dice R4 — Critical Hits and Ability Checks — Implementation Plan

**Status:** built 2026-10-26, accepted in Obsidian on 2026-10-26 ("es läuft alles") (the maintainer: "mach weiter, ich teste
später" after the quality round).

**Goal:** Two things R3 left out, small and inside the dice module: a critical hit's damage from a
damage button, and ability checks from a stat block's six abilities.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-dice-design.md` §7 (phase R4).

## Review Focus

1. **Critical damage** (`criticalFormula` in `src/dice/formula.ts`, pure): the SRD's rule — roll
   the attack's damage dice twice and add them, the numbers once: `1d10+5` → `2d10+5`,
   `2d8+1d6+3` → `4d8+2d6+3`. On a damage button: Shift-click rolls it, and the button's menu
   (right-click, long press) offers Normal / Critical hit. Label "Owlbear: Beak · damage ·
   critical", log `Owlbear rolls Beak damage (critical): 2d10+5 → 17 (4, 8, +5)`.
2. **Ability checks:** in every stat block view the modifier beside each score — `20 (+5)` — is a
   button rolling `d20+5` ("Owlbear: Strength check" / "Eulenbär: Stärkewurf"); Shift, Alt and the
   menu as for saves. While editing, the score stays editable on click; only the modifier rolls.

## Calls I made without the maintainer (check these)

- A critical hit is chosen by the GM (Shift or the menu); a natural 20 on the attack does not roll
  the damage by itself.
- Ability checks for characters come only from a stat block in their note (no new frontmatter key).
- Hidden GM rolls stay out: the maintainer did not choose them.
